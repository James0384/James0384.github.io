Site privacy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This note covers spinlightproductions.com, the website of Spin Light Productions LLC. The Receta Studio app has its own Receta Privacy Policy and Receta Terms.

The Start a project form

When you send the form on Start a project, it collects your name, your email address, the one-sentence project description you type, the website type, and whether the site is for a business or personal use. The form is delivered to us by email through Web3Forms, a form-handling service.

We use what you send only to reply to you about your project. We don’t sell it or add you to a mailing list.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t track you across other sites.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any website, these services process standard request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site.

Contact

Questions about this note, or want us to delete a message you sent? Email james.miller@spinlightproductions.com.
